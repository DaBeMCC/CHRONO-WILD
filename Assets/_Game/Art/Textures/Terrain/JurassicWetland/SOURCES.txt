Fifteen source maps at 4K were verified against saved Poly Haven file catalogs.
Source files, URLs and checksums: ArtSource/JurassicWetland/ground_sources.json.
Albedo and normal maps are exported at 4K. HeightAO JPG channels are
R=height, G=ambient occlusion, B=roughness. Terrain masks at 2K use
R=metallic (zero), G=AO, B=height, A=smoothness.

WetlandReflection.exr is an offline capture of the CHRONO WILD review scene.
New fern, horsetail and ginkgo sapling meshes are original Blender models.
They represent living/ancient plant groups for an artistic Jurassic study,
not a certified reconstruction of specific fossil species.
